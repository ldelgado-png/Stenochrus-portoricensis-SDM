The corresponding data and reproducible R scripts are located at [results/altitude](../../results/altitude), [R/12_altitudinal_analysis.R](../../R/12_altitudinal_analysis.R), and [R/13_altitudinal_figure.R](../../R/13_altitudinal_figure.R).
